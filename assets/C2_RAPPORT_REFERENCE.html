Technician digital sensitivity / Sensibilité numérique technicien

Reference convention used for cartography: 3 mm EDM reference = 50% FSH. Blind detection remains independent; the %FSH normalization is applied afterwards for technician interpretation.

Online report / Rapport en ligne

The report is generated from the same Twin context as geometry, encoded positions, focal laws, scan, detection, TFM and voxel. It is therefore part of the Living Engineering chain rather than a document added after the inspection.

Hardware-facing engineering data / Données d'ingénierie vers le hardware

The Twin exports PA position/orientation, nominal path and TOF, sector/skew definition and per-element focal-law delays. Final instrument-specific wedge delay, coupling, voltage and acquisition timing require physical validation on the selected equipment.

Evidence continuity / Continuité des preuves

B001 historical evidence / Preuve historique B001: preserved as a previous validated digital evidence layer for comparison and traceability. It is not substituted for the current Fusion-driven B014 scan. Current B014 detection is reconstructed from Fusion B009 data.

Engineering outputs / Livrables

  • exportable focal laws;
  • indication detection and analysis;
  • TFM and 3D voxel characterization;
  • decision matrix;
  • engineering report;
  • reusable digital context;
  • transfer basis for real inspection equipment and scanner.

Final hardware coupling, wedge transmission and absolute amplitude remain subject to physical validation.
